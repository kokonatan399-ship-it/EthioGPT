EthioGPT is an AI assistant designed for people in Ethiopia and anyone interested in Ethiopian languages, culture, learning, business, technology and everyday questions.

Features:
- AI chat
- English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo and Tigrinya language selection
- Image Studio
- Read-aloud voice features
- Ethiopian-focused library and project tools
- Simple mobile-first interface
